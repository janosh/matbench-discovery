The [DynaMat reference set](/benchmarks/md#test-set) spans pure metals, alloys, high-entropy alloys, transition-metal dichalcogenides, perovskites and molecular crystals at finite temperature; contributions expanding its coverage are welcome. The public reference data intentionally omits energies and forces. Evaluate your model with the shared runner [`models/run_md.py`](https://github.com/janosh/matbench-discovery/blob/main/models/run_md.py) and submit its metrics via [PR](https://github.com/janosh/matbench-discovery/blob/HEAD/contributing.md).

See [arXiv:2607.03433](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03433) for details on the MD task, the DynaMat reference set and the CMDS metric.
